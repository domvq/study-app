How it works

  1. Input: type a topic, paste notes, or upload a .txt / .md / .pdf document.
  2. Search: StudyAI looks up the topic on Wikipedia and combines that summary with your own material.
  3. Generate: it builds flashcards, a slideshow, a quiz, a study guide, expert-style step-by-step solutions, and a Spark Notes-style summary — all from the same source text, so they stay consistent.
  4. Study: flip cards, take the quiz, present the slideshow, or export the whole pack as Markdown.

Built as a static site on purpose: no server, no database, no build step. That is what makes it deployable on GitHub Pages in minutes. Your documents never leave your browser unless you choose to use an external AI API.
